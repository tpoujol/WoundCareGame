## What is Markdown?
Markdown, is simple lightweight markup language allowing the author to format plain text in a quick and easy way.

For example, if you want to create a title, you simply need to add the following sign at the beginning of the line `#` followed by a space. The number of sharp signs indicate the level of the title: 
- 1 for a main title
- 2 for a secondary
- 3 for a third level
- And so on

As you can see, you can also create bullet, or numbered lists simply using the `-` or the `1.`, `2.` sign at the beginning of the line
